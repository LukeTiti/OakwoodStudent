Privacy Policy

Oakwood Advisor – Veracross Bridge (Chrome extension)

What it accesses

This extension is used only by Oakwood School advisors to review student community service submissions. It reads pending submissions from the school's Firestore database — student name, email, hours, reflections, and supervisor signature/contact info — and, when an advisor approves one, posts the hours to Veracross using that advisor's own logged-in session.

How it's used

  • Solely to display pending submissions and let an advisor approve or reject them.
  • Never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Never used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Storage

Data lives in the school's Google Firebase project and is only ever shown inside this extension or the related Oakwood Students App and advisor tools.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the Oakwood Students App developer through the app itself.
